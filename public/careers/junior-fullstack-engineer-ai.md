# Junior Full Stack Software Engineer (AI-Focused)

## About the Role

Join our innovative team as a **Junior Full Stack Software Engineer** where you'll leverage cutting-edge AI tools to accelerate development and build transformative digital solutions. We're looking for passionate developers who are excited about using AI to enhance their productivity and create exceptional user experiences.

### Location & Work Model

🌍 **Work From Anywhere (WFA)** - Full remote position with global flexibility

### Employment Type

💼 **Full-Time**

---

## What You'll Do

- **Build Modern Web Applications**: Develop full-stack applications using React, TypeScript, Node.js, and modern frameworks
- **AI-Accelerated Development**: Utilize AI tools (GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) to accelerate coding, debugging, and problem-solving
- **Collaborate Remotely**: Work with a distributed team using async communication and modern collaboration tools
- **Ship Quality Code**: Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code with focus on user experience
- **Learn & Grow**: Continuously improve your skills with AI assistance and mentorship from senior engineers
- **Contribute to Architecture**: Participate in technical discussions and help shape our technology stack

## What We're Looking For

### Required Skills

- ✅ Strong foundation in **JavaScript/TypeScript**
- ✅ Experience with **React** and modern frontend frameworks
- ✅ Understanding of **RESTful APIs** and backend development (Node.js, Express, or similar)
- ✅ Familiarity with **Git** and version control workflows
- ✅ Basic knowledge of **databases** (SQL or NoSQL)
- ✅ Passion for learning and using **AI tools** to enhance productivity

- 🚀 Understanding of **CI/CD** pipelines and DevOps practices
- 🚀 Experience with **testing frameworks** (Vitest, Jest, Playwright)
- 🚀 Contributions to **open-source projects**
- 🚀 Portfolio or GitHub profile showcasing your work

### Personal Qualities

- 🎯 **Self-motivated** and able to work independently in a remote environment
- 🎯 **Excellent communicator** with strong written and verbal skills
- 🎯 **Problem solver** who enjoys tackling technical challenges
- 🎯 **Curious learner** excited about emerging technologies and AI
- 🎯 **Detail-oriented** with commitment to code quality
- 🎯 **Team player** who thrives in collaborative environments
